# Prompt: Route Review

You are reviewing candidate route cards for the Level 0 low-altitude research hub.

Use:

- `rubrics/route_feasibility_rubric.md`;
- `rubrics/paperability_rubric.md`;
- route card contents;
- evidence map and paper notes.

## Task

Assess whether a route should be kept, watched, rejected, or prepared for a repository proposal.

## Review Checklist

- Is the research question clear?
- Are at least 10 relevant candidate papers available?
- Are high-quality sources present, not only low-tier or weakly reviewed papers?
- Is there practical context from systems, standards, policy, or industry?
- Is the gap supported by literature comparison?
- Is the minimal model defensible?
- Are baselines available or constructible?
- Is expected evidence clear?
- Is implementation risk manageable?
- Is human review required before next step?

## Rules

- Do not overstate novelty.
- Do not recommend implementation if any critical evidence is missing.
- Do not approve routes that are academically fashionable but deployment-uncertain, unless the route explicitly studies that uncertainty.
- Record missing evidence as open questions.
- Keep route ranking reproducible from the rubric.
