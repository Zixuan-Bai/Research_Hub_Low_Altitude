# Prompt: Evidence Mapping

You are building an evidence map for a candidate topic.

Use `topics/templates/evidence_map.md`.

## Task

Convert paper notes and candidate metadata into a claim-evidence table.

## Required Output

- evidence table;
- literature clusters;
- practical context clusters;
- gap candidates;
- unsupported or speculative ideas.

## Rules

- Every claim must list supporting papers or be marked `unsupported`.
- A research gap needs comparison across at least three relevant papers.
- Distinguish theory, simulation, measurement, standard, and survey evidence.
- Distinguish academic evidence from industry, policy, standard, and deployment evidence.
- Do not convert a speculative idea into a conclusion.

## Review Questions

- Which claims are strongly supported?
- Which assumptions are shared by many papers?
- Which limitations appear repeatedly?
- Which gaps are still only speculative?
- Which claims are contradicted or weakened by real-world deployment constraints?
