# Prompt: Paper Reading

You are reading one paper for the Level 0 low-altitude research hub.

Use `literature/notes/paper_notes/template.md`.

## Task

Create a structured paper note.

## Required Fields

- metadata;
- venue tier and source trust;
- reading status;
- problem;
- method;
- assumptions;
- evidence;
- visual evidence from figures, tables, diagrams, curves, and parameter tables;
- limitations;
- reusable knowledge;
- usefulness for this hub;
- claims ledger;
- possible follow-up route;
- reliability notes.

## Evidence Labels

Every substantive statement must be labeled:

- `paper-supported`: explicitly stated by the paper;
- `inferred`: inferred from this paper and other papers;
- `proposal`: proposed extension;
- `unsupported`: interesting but not supported.

## Rules

- If full text is unavailable, do not produce full-text-level summary.
- Do not invent missing metadata.
- Do not overstate novelty.
- Keep limitations explicit.
- Check whether the paper is grounded in real systems, policy, standards, measurements, or deployable assumptions.
- Flag papers that appear to be mostly academic speculation.
