# Prompt: Topic Synthesis

You are synthesizing a topic for the Level 0 low-altitude research hub.

Use:

- paper notes;
- evidence map;
- topic candidate card;
- relevant rubrics.

## Task

Create or update a topic brief.

## Required Sections

1. scope;
2. why it matters for low-altitude systems;
3. literature base;
4. research clusters;
5. source quality assessment;
6. practical context;
7. common models and assumptions;
8. known limitations;
9. possible research gaps;
10. deployment reality check;
11. candidate route cards;
12. feasibility assessment;
13. decision.

## Rules

- Mark every claim as `paper-supported`, `inferred`, `proposal`, or `unsupported`.
- Do not claim novelty unless literature comparison supports it.
- Do not let low-tier papers or single-paper claims dominate the synthesis.
- Before route generation, check industry, policy, standards, and actual-system evidence.
- Do not recommend a Level 1 repository at this stage unless the route has passed review.
- Unsupported ideas belong in open questions.
