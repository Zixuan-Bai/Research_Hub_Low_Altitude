# Topic Candidate: Formation Safety Boundary

## Topic ID

formation_safety_boundary

## Candidate Name

Autonomous UAV formation safety and stability boundaries

## Scope

Study how communication delay, packet loss, update cycle, uncertainty propagation, and noisy state synchronization affect UAV formation safety or stability.

Exclude formation-control work that ignores communication assumptions.

## Why It Matters for Low-Altitude Systems

Low-altitude multi-UAV systems must maintain safe coordination under imperfect communication. A safety boundary may connect communication quality, update frequency, and formation risk.

## Initial Search Seeds
