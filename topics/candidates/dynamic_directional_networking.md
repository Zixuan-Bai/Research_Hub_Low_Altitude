# Topic Candidate: Dynamic Directional Networking

## Topic ID

dynamic_directional_networking

## Candidate Name

Dynamic directional networking for low-altitude aerial networks

## Scope

Study directional transmission, dynamic aerial topology, clustering, cluster-head coordination, beam selection, interference suppression, and spatial reuse.

Exclude pure antenna hardware work without networking or topology implications.

## Why It Matters for Low-Altitude Systems

Directional communication may improve spatial reuse and interference management in dense aerial networks, but mobility and topology uncertainty make coordination difficult.
