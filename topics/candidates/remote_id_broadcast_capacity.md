# Topic Candidate: Remote ID Broadcast Capacity

## Topic ID

remote_id_broadcast_capacity

## Candidate Name

UAV broadcast capacity analysis / Remote ID-like broadcast systems

## Scope

Study high-density UAV direct broadcast, Remote ID-like packet dissemination, packet reception probability, hidden terminals, channel congestion, and density-related safety boundaries.

Exclude pure regulation summaries that do not include a communication, capacity, interference, or safety model.

## Why It Matters for Low-Altitude Systems

Low-altitude airspace may require many UAVs to broadcast identity, state, or safety messages. The key question is whether direct broadcast remains reliable as density and broadcast frequency increase.
