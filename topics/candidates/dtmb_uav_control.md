# Topic Candidate: DTMB UAV Control

## Topic ID

dtmb_uav_control

## Candidate Name

DTMB-based or terrestrial-broadcast-based large-scale UAV management

## Scope

Study one-to-many broadcast control, command dissemination, coverage, latency, reliability, uplink feedback, fallback behavior, and relation to UTM/U-space.

Exclude proposals that only mention broadcast infrastructure without technical control, reliability, or network modeling.

## Why It Matters for Low-Altitude Systems

Large-scale low-altitude management may need efficient downlink dissemination to many UAVs. Terrestrial broadcast systems could be relevant if their coverage and latency properties match command/control requirements.

## Initial Search Seeds

- DTMB
- digital terrestrial multimedia broadcasting
- UAV command and control
- broadcast control channel
- low altitude air traffic management
- cellular broadcast UAV control
- multicast UAV control

## Existing User Foundation

Potential connection to prior work on broadcast cycles, UAV network control, and safety fallback. This connection needs evidence.

## Evidence Needed

- Broadcast architecture papers.
- Latency and reliability models.
- Comparisons with cellular broadcast/multicast.
- Uplink feedback and safety fallback mechanisms.

## Open Questions

- Is DTMB technically defensible as a research object, or should the topic generalize to terrestrial broadcast/multicast?
- What measurable advantage would be tested?
- Are public technical models available?
