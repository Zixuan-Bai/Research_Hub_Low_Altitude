# Topic Potential Rubric

Score each topic candidate from 0 to 5.

## 1. Low-Altitude Fit

Does the topic directly address low-altitude communication, control, autonomy, or safety?

## 2. Literature Availability

Are there enough technical papers to support a survey and route cards?

## 3. Gap Visibility

Can possible gaps be inferred from literature comparison rather than speculation?

## 4. Modeling Clarity

Is there a plausible minimal model?

## 5. Baseline Availability

Are baselines available or constructible?

## 6. Fit With User Foundation

Does it connect to prior work on uncertainty, safety boundaries, broadcast cycles, UAV networks, reinforcement learning, graph models, or planning?

## Decision

- `continue surveying`: promising but not enough evidence.
- `generate route cards`: enough evidence and gap candidates.
- `pause`: unclear fit or thin literature.
- `reject`: weak fit and no clear evidence path.
