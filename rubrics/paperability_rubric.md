# Paperability Rubric

This rubric estimates whether a route could plausibly become a paper. It does not prove novelty.

## 1. Problem Importance

Is the problem meaningful for low-altitude communication, autonomy, or safety?

## 2. Evidence Path

Are the expected figures, tables, proofs, or simulations clear?

## 3. Differentiation

Can the route be positioned against recent literature?

## 4. Scope Control

Can the route be finished within a manageable 3-6 month research cycle?

## 5. Writing Path

Is there a plausible paper outline with related work, method, experiments, and limitations?

## Labels

- `strong`: likely worth deeper investigation.
- `medium`: needs more literature or modeling clarity.
- `weak`: keep as watch item or reject.

Do not use this rubric to claim acceptance likelihood.
